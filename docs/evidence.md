## 20 November 2026 — SH refusal cause verified: 40% single-security concentration cap

The official participation handbook (`https://moomoo-qoder-challenge-rgkelnyuzbm.qoder.website/`, fetched 20 Nov 00:21Z) states under "Moomoo Paper Trading Account Terms & Conditions", §3 Trading and Position Limits:

- **"Exposure to stock/ETF may not exceed 40% of total initial account assets."** The limit applies where a purchase would push a position above 40%; it does not apply when exceeded by price movement or options exercise.
- Eligible products: **US-listed stocks, ETFs and options only**. No restriction on inverse or leveraged ETFs is stated. Trading sessions: 04:00–20:00 ET.
- Other limits: stocks under USD 1 ineligible; companies under USD 500M market cap ineligible; max 200 executed orders/day; max 100 positions; forced liquidation if liquidity goes below zero.

Classification of the five refusals: **`VERIFIED_CONCENTRATION_LIMIT`**. The sanctioned order BUY 1,764 SH ≈ USD 59.5–60.1k ≈ 60% of the ~USD 100k account, above the 40% cap (~USD 40k). The filled QQQ diagnostic (1 share ≈ 0.66%) was below it. Ledger comparison shows the broker-bound request envelopes for QQQ (filled) and SH (refused) are identical in every field except symbol, quantity and reference price — same transport, account fingerprint, order type and time-in-force. `ret_code=-5` / `ret_msg='backend business error'` was the broker's generic surface for this business rule; no support inquiry is needed.

Maximum compliant SH entry at the 19 Nov quote ≈ **1,173 shares ≈ USD 40k ≈ −40% S&P exposure** (vs the risk-v2 MODERATE tier's −60% target via SH). The strategy/policy adjustment is proposed to the owner, not applied silently; the support-inquiry draft is superseded.

## Verification levels

1. **Implemented** — code exists and is covered by the repository's own tests, but has not been observed against an external system.
2. **Automated-test verified** — unit/fixture/mock tests executed locally with observed results.
3. **Genuine Qoder run** — a completed Qoder Cloud Agent session set, with recorded session ids and artifacts.
4. **Real Moomoo account read** — an authenticated read-only call against the competition paper account.
5. **Broker-confirmed paper order** — an order submitted to and confirmed by the broker, with fills reconciled. **The 18 November QQQ diagnostic BUY and SELL both filled and reconciled; no strategy fill is claimed.**
6. **Blocked / unverified** — claimed capability without supporting observation.
